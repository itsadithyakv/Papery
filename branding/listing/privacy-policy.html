Papery privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Papery doesn't collect any data. Your files never leave your PC.

Your files

Papery opens, changes and saves files only on your computer. It doesn't upload them anywhere, and there's no account or cloud service.

Network use

The only thing Papery connects to is the Microsoft Store, to show the price of Papery Pro, handle a purchase and check that you own it. Microsoft handles that under its own privacy statement. Papery never sees your payment details.

What stays on your PC

To work properly, Papery keeps these in its own private folder on your PC: your settings, your recent files list and the page you were on, the signatures you save (encrypted with your Windows account), a record that you own Papery Pro, a local log of errors, and temporary recovery copies of work you haven't saved. These are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Papery removes them.

Camera and scanner

Papery uses your camera or scanner only when you choose to scan a page, and only to make that page. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the temporary picture is deleted straight after.

No analytics, no ads

Papery contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Children

Papery collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Questions are welcome at YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL.
